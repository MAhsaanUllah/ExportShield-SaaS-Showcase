  ENGLISH SETUP GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STEP 1 — EXTRACT
  Extract the ZIP file to any folder on your computer.
  (Recommended: Desktop or C:\Programs\ExportShield)

STEP 2 — RUN
  Double-click 'ExportShield_Pro.exe'.
  Windows may show a SmartScreen warning — click "More info"
  then "Run anyway". This is expected for unsigned software.

STEP 3 — GET YOUR ACTIVATION KEY
  The software will show your unique "System ID" on screen.
  Copy it and WhatsApp it to:

      Ahsaan Ullah — ExportShield Support
      WhatsApp: [Your Number Here]

STEP 4 — ACTIVATE
  Paste the Activation Key you receive into the input field.
  Click "Activate Software".
  The software will unlock immediately and remember your
  activation — you will NOT need to re-enter it again.

STEP 5 — START INVOICING
  Go to Company Settings > Enter your VAT Number and Company Name.
  Upload your Excel file on the Dashboard.
  Generate ZATCA-compliant PDF invoices in one click.

  NOTE: All data stays 100% on your computer. Nothing is
  sent to the internet. Your invoices are yours only.

QADAM 1 — EXTRACT KAREIN
  ZIP file ko apne computer ke kisi bhi folder mein extract
  karein. (Behtar hai: Desktop ya C:\Programs\ExportShield)

QADAM 2 — CHALAYEIN
  'ExportShield_Pro.exe' par double-click karein.
  Agar Windows SmartScreen warning aaye, to "More info"
  press karein, phir "Run anyway" click karein.

QADAM 3 — ACTIVATION KEY HASIL KAREIN
  Software screen par apka unique "System ID" dikhayega.
  Uss ID ko copy karein aur WhatsApp karein:

      Ahsaan Ullah — ExportShield Support
      WhatsApp: [Your Number Here]

QADAM 4 — ACTIVATE KAREIN
  Jo Activation Key milegi, usse software ke box mein paste
  karein. "Activate Software" button dabayein.
  Software fauran unlock ho jayega aur activation yaad
  rakhega — dobara enter karne ki zaroorat nahi.

QADAM 5 — INVOICING SHURU KAREIN
  Company Settings mein jaayein > VAT Number aur Company
  Name enter karein. Dashboard par apni Excel file upload
  karein. Ek click mein ZATCA-compliant PDF invoices generate
  ho jayenge.

  NOTE: Aapka sara data 100% aapke computer par rehta hai.
  Internet par kuch nahi bheja jaata. Aapki invoices sirf
  aapki hain.
